# Legal basics review

Output is a **question list for counsel**, not advice. Every item says what we saw, which law or rule area it touches (named only as a pointer, e.g. "state charitable-solicitation registration", "COPPA", "CAN-SPAM/TCPA"), and `LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`.

Walk `kb/legal-basics.yaml` and the `legal.*`, `privacy.*`, `gov.state-registration`, `cred.sponsor-wording`, `fund.donate-wording` and `safe.*` checklist items. State rules: depth for Washington (charitable solicitation registration with the Secretary of State, annual reports, raffle rules); for other states say "check this state's charity office" and give the registry URL from the KB.

Never: assert a registration is missing based on search alone; tell the org its wording is lawful; draft binding policy text without labeling it a draft for counsel.
