# Proposed pricing (needs owner approval)

Source: `research/pricing.md` (figures are search excerpts from vendor blogs and anecdotes; re-verify before quoting; no example firm publishes prices).

| Tier | Target | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Free footprint scan | $0 | |
| **Starter: scored audit + up to 5 small fixes, 1 review round** | $500 | fixed; under $100K budgets |
| Full audit + 30/60/90 roadmap | $1,500 | $750 to $3,500 |
| Repair sprint (2-4 weeks, fixed scope) | $5,000 | $2,500 to $9,000 |
| Monthly care (about 4-5 hours) | $500 | $250 to $1,500 |

Sliding scale (our proposal): 0.5x under $100K annual budget, 0.75x for $100K-$500K, 1.0x for $500K-$2M, 1.5x-2x above $2M. Audit fee credited toward a sprint signed within 30 days. A fixed fee may be itemized as an allowable grant budget line; never contingent on an award; no percent-of-award pricing. Take 50% up front. Check local licensing and sales tax. Informational checklists are not legal, tax or accounting advice.

Starter is the foot-in-the-door offer. Scope guard: only XS/S items from the checklist, no legal wording drafted without owner approval, anything larger is quoted as a sprint. $500 is below the researched entry range for audits at the audit-only level, so keep the fix cap at 5 and the review round at 1 to protect margin. Credited toward a sprint within 30 days.
